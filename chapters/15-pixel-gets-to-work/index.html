Now you know the grown-up words

How AI systems write code, operate computers, and execute complex workflows:

🤖 Agentic AI

A goal-driven AI system that plans, uses external tools, and iterates autonomously until a task is done.

🛠️ Tool Use (Function Calling)

Allowing an AI to trigger real actions (like running code, sending emails, or browsing websites).

🔄 Action-Observation Loop

The cycle of: Inspect environment → Take an action → Observe the result → Decide what to do next.

🛑 Autonomy (Stopping Condition)

Knowing when the goal has been successfully accomplished and terminating without human intervention.
