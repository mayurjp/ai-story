Now you know the grown-up words

Tap one to see what it means.
Agentic AI +
Everything before this chapter, Pixel only ever answered a question in one shot. Here it does something different: it checks the plant, acts, checks again, and decides on its own when the job is done, across through . A system that plans and acts toward a goal like this, instead of just answering once, is called an agent — and this whole way of working is called agentic AI.
Tool use +
The watering can Pixel actually picks up in and is outside its own "brain" — Pixel doesn't water the plant by thinking about it, it reaches for a real tool and uses it. Reaching outside itself to use an actual tool to get something done is called tool use.
Action-observation loop +
Pixel doesn't act once and walk away — it acts, checks what changed, and decides what to do next based on that, repeating from through . Repeating this act-then-check cycle until a goal is met is called an action-observation loop.
Autonomy +
Nobody tells Pixel exactly when to stop watering in — it decides for itself, once the leaves perk back up, that the job is done. Making decisions and choosing when to stop without being told each individual step is called autonomy.
