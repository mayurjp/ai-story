A tricky pronoun puzzle

Look at this sentence: "The trophy didn't fit in the suitcase because it was too big." What is "it"? Is it the trophy or the suitcase?

Now you know the grown-up words

The core architecture behind all modern Large Language Models:

💡 Attention

Looking at every word in a sentence at the same time and calculating how much each word relates to the others.

🔦 Attention Weight

The thickness/strength of the connection beam (thick beam for "trophy", thin beam for "suitcase").

⚡ Transformer

The neural network architecture (invented at Google in 2017) built entirely out of self-attention layers.

🚀 Parallel Processing

Reading all words across an entire document simultaneously rather than one by one.
