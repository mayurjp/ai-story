Now you know the grown-up words

Tap one to see what it means.
Attention +
What Pixel does across , , and — instead of only looking at the word right next to "it," Pixel looks back at every word in the sentence and decides how much each one matters. Looking across everything at once like this, and deciding what matters, is called attention.
Attention weight +
The thickness of the line connecting "it" to each word in — a number saying exactly how much that word should count. Ball ends up with a heavy weight; box ends up with a light one. That number is called an attention weight.
Self-attention +
The specific thing happening in through : the words are weighing each other, all from within the same sentence — nothing from outside gets brought in to help. When a sentence looks at its own words to understand itself, that's called self-attention.
Transformer +
The name for the whole design built from stacking these attention-weighing steps together — the same idea you watched in this story, repeated in layers. This exact design, built for real in 2017, is called a transformer.
