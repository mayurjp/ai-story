Now you know the grown-up words

The physical hardware, economics, and edge compression powering modern AI:

🎮 GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)

A specialized chip with thousands of tiny cores designed to perform simple math multiplications in parallel.

⚡ Compute & FLOPs

The total raw mathematical power (Floating-Point Operations) available to train and scale AI models.

📈 Scaling Laws

The empirical law proving that combining more compute with more tokens reliably makes neural networks smarter.

📱 On-Device AI & Quantization (TinyML)

Compressing heavy 16-bit weights into lightweight 4-bit numbers so Pixel runs privately on a phone or smartwatch with zero internet.
