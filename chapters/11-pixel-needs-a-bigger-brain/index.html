Now you know the grown-up words

Tap one to see what it means.
Compute +
The raw amount of calculating power Pixel has to think with. It has very little in , and a huge amount once it's plugged in during . That raw calculating capacity is called compute.
GPU +
The actual kind of chip filling the machine room in — built to do huge numbers of small calculations at once, which happens to be exactly the kind of math weights and knobs need. That chip is called a GPU, or graphics processing unit — originally built for video games, now the workhorse of AI.
Data center +
The actual building full of racks of these chips, running day and night, that Pixel gets plugged into in . That building is called a data center.
Scaling +
What actually made Pixel faster in — not a cleverer idea, just far more compute and far more data to think with. Getting better mostly by adding more compute and data, rather than a smarter trick, is called scaling.
