Why AI makes mistakes and how modern 2025–2026 reasoning models fix them:

👻 Hallucination

When an AI generates fluent, convincing text that is factually false or physically impossible.

⚡ System 1 vs 🧠 System 2

System 1 is instant instinct (quick guessing); System 2 is slow, deliberate step-by-step reasoning.

⏳ Test-Time Compute (Reasoning Models)

Modern models (like OpenAI o1/o3, DeepSeek R1) spend extra seconds generating hidden thinking tokens before speaking.

🔄 Self-Correction & Backtracking

If an internal thought leads to an error during scratchpad reasoning, the model catches its own mistake and tries a better path.
