Now you know the grown-up words

Why AI makes mistakes and how scientists fix them:

👻 Hallucination

When an AI generates fluent, convincing text that is factually false or physically impossible.

⚡ System 1 vs 🧠 System 2

System 1 is instant instinct (quick guessing); System 2 is slow, deliberate step-by-step reasoning.

🔗 Chain of Thought (CoT)

Prompting the AI to write out its intermediate thinking steps before blurting out a final conclusion.

🌍 Grounding

Connecting word tokens to real-world physical laws, verified web searches, or databases.
