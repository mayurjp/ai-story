Now you know the grown-up words

Tap one to see what it means.
Hallucination +
What happens in — Pixel doesn't say "I don't know," it confidently states something invented that sounds right but isn't. That confidently-wrong kind of answer is called a hallucination.
System 1 +
The fast, instant kind of answering Pixel does in and — pattern-matching straight from everything it's read, without stopping to reason. That quick, automatic kind of thinking is called System 1.
System 2 +
The slower, deliberate kind of thinking Pixel switches to in — working through a problem step by step instead of blurting the first answer. That careful, step-by-step kind of thinking is called System 2.
Chain of thought +
The actual words Pixel would produce while reasoning slowly in , if it wrote them out loud: "a glass is fragile... it's about to hit a hard floor... fragile things hitting hard floors shatter." Writing the reasoning out step by step like this, instead of jumping straight to an answer, is called chain of thought.
Persistent memory +
The one thing doesn't fully fix: the next time someone starts a new conversation with Pixel, it won't remember any of this — every conversation starts over from scratch. Carrying memories from one conversation into the next is called persistent memory, and it's something Pixel, as you've met it here, doesn't have.
