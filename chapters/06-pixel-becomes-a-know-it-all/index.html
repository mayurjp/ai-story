Pixel Becomes a Know-It-All

How does an AI write whole essays, recipes, and stories? It doesn't write in one huge leap—it predicts the next word in an infinite loop: Large Language Models (LLMs).

"I write one word at a time!"
Step 1 of 5

AI writes one word at a time

When you ask ChatGPT a question, it doesn't think up the whole paragraph in advance. It writes one piece (token) at a time, guessing the most likely next word!

Now you know the grown-up words

The concepts that make ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude work:

🔤 Token

A bite-sized chunk of text (a word or part of a word) that the AI reads and writes.

🔄 Autoregressive Generation

The loop of predicting one token, adding it to the story, and predicting the next token based on everything written so far.

📚 LLM (Large Language Model)

A massive transformer network trained on trillions of words to master general conversation.

🎛️ Temperature (Creativity)

A setting controlling how predictable (low) or wild/creative (high) the next word choices are.
