Now you know the grown-up words

Tap one to see what it means.
Token +
Each of the small pieces filling in one at a time, starting in — a word or small chunk of a word. Each one of those pieces, produced one at a time, is called a token.
Autoregressive generation +
The way Pixel makes each new piece in and : it looks at everything written so far, not just the last piece, and picks the next one — over and over, each new piece feeding into the next round. That step-by-step, self-feeding pattern is called autoregressive generation.
Fine-tuning +
What happens in — after Pixel already knows a huge amount from all its reading, a little extra practice with feedback on which answers are actually helpful sharpens its choices for a specific job. That feedback-guided practice on top of everything already read is called fine-tuning.
Large language model +
The name for the whole system built by putting all of this together — weighing words, producing one piece at a time, sharpened by feedback — trained on an enormous amount of text, as you watched across this whole story. That's what people mean by a large language model, or LLM.
