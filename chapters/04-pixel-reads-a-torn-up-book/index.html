The internet has too many books

Humans cannot write test questions for billions of internet sentences. So scientists let AI teach itself using a simple trick: hide a word, and make the AI guess it!

Now you know the grown-up words

How modern AI trains on the entire internet:

📖 Self-Supervised Learning

Training on unlabelled data by having the data supervise itself (hiding words and checking if guesses match the original text).

⬛ Masking

Blotting out 15% of the words in a sentence so the model must use surrounding context clues to guess what was hidden.

🧩 Context

The surrounding words in a sentence that give meaning to the hidden piece (like "sat" + "mat" hinting at "cat").

🏗️ Pretraining

Reading billions of masked sentences across Wikipedia and books to build a massive foundation of general knowledge.

🤔 What happens in long sentences where words are far apart?

In a sentence like "The dog who chased the cat into the garden was tired", "dog" and "tired" are 8 words apart! Reading left-to-right forgets the start. That's why we need the Attention Mechanism in Chapter 6!
