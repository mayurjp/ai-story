Now you know the grown-up words

Tap one to see what it means.
Bias +
What's going on in — Chip isn't trying to be unfair, but extra copies of one name in what it read left it leaning one way, every single time, without meaning to. That unfair lean, coming from unevenness in what a system learned from, is called bias.
Red-teaming +
The tricky, repeated questions people throw at Chip on purpose in — hunting for exactly the kind of unfair pick that stays hidden until pushed. Deliberately trying to break or trick a system to find its problems first is called red-teaming.
Alignment +
The goal behind fixing Chip in and — making sure its answers actually match what people intend and value, not just whatever looked most common in training. This ongoing work of shaping a system's behavior to match what people actually want is called alignment.
AI safety +
The umbrella name for all of this — catching bias, red-teaming for hidden problems, checking alignment — so machines like Chip and Pixel behave the way people actually need them to. This whole area of work is called AI safety.
