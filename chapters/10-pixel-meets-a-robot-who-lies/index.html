Now you know the grown-up words

How engineers keep AI systems fair, safe, and aligned with human values:

⚖️ AI Bias

When an algorithm systematically favors or disadvantages groups due to historical imbalances in training data.

🎯 Alignment

Ensuring an AI's goals and outputs reflect human ethics, truthfulness, and safety principles.

🕵️ Red-Teaming

Employing friendly safety testers to deliberately prompt the model with tricky attacks to discover flaws before launch.

🛡️ Guardrails

Input/output safety filters that block harmful, toxic, or discriminatory content in real time.
