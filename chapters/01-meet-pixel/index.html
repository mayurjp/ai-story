Pixel starts with zero knowledge

Pixel is shown a fresh Cookie 🍪. But Pixel's "Chocolate Chip Dial" is set to 0. Since Pixel ignores the clue, its meter stays empty and it blurts out the wrong guess: "Is it a Rock? 🪨"

Now you know the grown-up words

Here is what computer scientists call what you just watched:

🔍 Input (The Clue)

The information Pixel inspects (e.g. "Are there chocolate chips?").

🎛️ Weight (The Dial)

How much Pixel cares about a clue. Turning the dial up makes that clue powerful.

🔔 Threshold (The Bell Line)

The score needed to decide. Crossing the threshold rings the bell!

🧠 Neuron (The Whole Machine)

Clue × Dial → Check Threshold → Output answer. That is an Artificial Neuron!

🤔 Where does 1 neuron break down?

What if someone shows Pixel a chocolate-covered pebble? A single dial can't tell them apart! That's why real AI stacks hundreds of neurons into layers to check multiple clues at once (Chapter 3).
