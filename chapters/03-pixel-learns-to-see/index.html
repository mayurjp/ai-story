The photo is overwhelming

Pixel is handed a photo of a Cat 🐱. A photo has millions of tiny colored dots (pixels). Trying to connect wires to every single pixel burns out Pixel's brain!

Now you know the grown-up words

How computers see images, medical scans, and self-driving roads:

🔍 Convolution

Sliding a small patch filter across an entire picture to scan for patterns everywhere.

🔲 Filter / Kernel

A tiny pattern detector designed to search for specific clues like lines, curves, or textures.

🗺️ Feature Map

A summary map showing where in the picture each feature (ears, whiskers, eyes) was found.

👁️ Computer Vision (CNN)

Stacking feature maps in layers to recognize objects (Edges → Shapes → Faces → Cats!).
