Starting from pure static

You ask Pixel to paint: "A cat in a space suit 🐱🚀." Unlike a human painter who draws outlines first, Pixel starts with a canvas filled with 100% pure random TV static (noise)!

Now you know the grown-up words

How Midjourney, DALL-E, and Sora create images and videos:

🎨 Diffusion Model

An AI model that generates images by starting with random noise and gradually removing the noise step-by-step.

🧹 Denoising

The mathematical operation of predicting and subtracting random pixel noise to reveal clean underlying shapes.

✍️ Text Conditioning (CLIP)

Using text embeddings to steer the denoising process toward the user's prompt (e.g. "cat" + "space suit").

🎬 Latent Space

A compressed mathematical canvas where the AI sculpts concepts before rendering the final high-res pixels.
