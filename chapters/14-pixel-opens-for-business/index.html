How raw AI models get polished into finished products:

📚 Base Model

The raw AI loaded with internet knowledge, before any conversational or safety shaping.

🎯 Instruction Fine-Tuning

Teaching the model how to follow commands and format answers like a helpful assistant.

👍 RLHF (Human Feedback)

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback: Human testers rank answers to reward helpfulness and honesty.

🚀 Deployment

Hosting the polished, safe model in high-speed cloud servers so millions of users can chat with it.
