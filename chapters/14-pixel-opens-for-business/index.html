Now you know the grown-up words

Tap one to see what it means.
Base model +
Pixel in — after all that cookbook-reading, but before ever meeting a real customer. It knows a huge amount, but hasn't been shaped for any one job yet. That starting-point version, loaded with knowledge but not yet specialized, is called a base model.
RLHF +
The rating-and-adjusting loop in : real people rate different dishes, and Pixel's recipe shifts toward whatever got rated best. Learning from a stream of human ratings like this is called RLHF — reinforcement learning from human feedback.
Deployment +
What happens in — the doors open, and people outside the training kitchen actually get to use what Pixel makes. Releasing a trained system for real people to use is called deployment.
