Now you know the grown-up words

Tap one to see what it means.
Applied AI +
What's happening by : the same underlying skills — seeing, listening, predicting — that Pixel learned earlier get pointed at real jobs, from reading scans to spotting shady bank transactions. Putting a trained system's skills to work on one specific real-world job like this is called applied AI.
Domain data +
The thousands of X-rays doctors already marked, that Pixel studies in . Examples drawn from one specific field of work, used to sharpen a general skill for that one job, are called domain data.
Human-in-the-loop +
What's still true even after : Pixel doesn't make the final call alone — it flags the shadow, and a real doctor still decides. Keeping a person checking or making the final decision alongside the machine is called human-in-the-loop.
Computer-aided diagnosis +
The specific name for what Pixel does in — using a trained pattern-spotter to help doctors notice things in a scan they might otherwise miss. This exact use is called computer-aided diagnosis.
