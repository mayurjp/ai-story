Pixel Takes a Shot

When you miss a basketball hoop, how do you fix your aim? Pixel learns to work backward from its mistakes—the fundamental breakthrough behind deep learning: Backpropagation.

🤖
"I want to shoot a basket!"
Step 1 of 5

Shooting with multiple body parts

Shooting a basketball isn't just 1 simple knob. Pixel has 3 joints in a row (layers): Shoulder angle → Elbow bend → Wrist snap. How does Pixel know which joint made the mistake?

Now you know the grown-up words

Here is the engine behind all modern deep learning:

🎯 Loss (The Mistake)

How far off the guess was from the goal (e.g. missing the hoop by 30 centimeters).

🔙 Backpropagation

Working backward from the error at the end to figure out how much each joint/layer contributed to the miss.

📉 Gradient Descent

Nudging each joint by a tiny amount in the exact direction that reduces the error.

🧱 Multi-Layer Network

Stacking neurons in sequential layers (Shoulder → Elbow → Wrist) to learn complex non-linear actions.

🤔 What happens when Pixel needs to SEE things?

A photo has millions of pixels. Stacking joints one by one is too slow! To recognize faces and animals, Pixel needs a sliding filter—a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) in Chapter 4!
