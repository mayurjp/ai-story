Now you know the grown-up words

Tap one to see what it means.
World model +
What Pixel builds in — an internal picture of how things move and interact, not just which words tend to follow each other. Having this kind of internal picture of how the world behaves is called a world model.
Planning +
What Pixel is doing by — using its world model to picture not just the very next moment, but several moves ahead. Working out a sequence of future steps like this, before anything happens, is called planning.
Hierarchical prediction +
Also happening by : Pixel predicts at more than one scale at once — "the ball will reach the top soon" (the big picture) and "the ball is moving forward right now" (the small detail), both at the same time. Predicting at multiple zoom levels like this is called hierarchical prediction.
JEPA +
The name for this exact kind of learning — building an internal picture of the world and predicting what happens next in it, the way Pixel does starting in — rather than just predicting the next word. This design is called a Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture, or JEPA for short.
