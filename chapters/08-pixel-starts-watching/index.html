Beyond just reading words

Humans only read ~100 million words in a lifetime, yet a 3-year-old child understands physics better than an AI model trained on a trillion words! Why? Because children learn by watching real video streams of the world.

Now you know the grown-up words

How AI learns common-sense physics and visual reasoning:

🌍 World Model

An internal mental simulation of how the physical world works (gravity, friction, 3D space, momentum).

🎥 Video Prediction

Training an AI by showing it past frames of a video and making it predict the next visual state.

🧠 JEPA (Joint Embedding Architecture)

Predicting abstract concepts (the apple lands on the floor) rather than wasting energy predicting every noisy background pixel.

🧩 Common Sense

Intuitive physical knowledge (liquid pours down, solid objects don't pass through walls).
